## A new chapter

I'm pleased to announce that I've been hired by <a href="https://opengis.ch" target="_blank">**OpenGIS.ch**</a> as a GIS developer.
It's hard for me to imagine a better landing spot at this point in my career. OpenGIS.ch is one of the teams that maintain and push forward open-source building blocks that a large part of the GIS ecosystem relies on: QGIS, QGIS Server, QField, QFieldCloud. After years spent assembling these tools into business applications, I can't wait to work alongside the developers of this software, even if I'll obviously stay more on web-oriented projects.

As for <a href="https://github.com/0live/Canopy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Canopy</a>, I'll try to keep moving it forward because this project matters to me, but since I have interests in life beyond sitting behind a computer, I honestly don't know how much time I'll be able to devote to it!
To be continued.
